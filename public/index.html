Common Queries

Does it work across different OS?

Yes. Aether is browser-native. You can share files between an iPhone, an Android tablet, a Windows PC, and a Mac—as long as they are on the same Wi-Fi network.

Is there any data logging?

Zero. Since the connection is P2P (Peer-to-Peer), your files move directly between devices. Our signaling server only helps the devices "find" each other—it never sees or stores your data.

What if the network is public?

Aether is designed for security. The PIN-based handshake ensures that even on a public Wi-Fi, only the person you authorize can establish a link and receive your stream.

Do I need high-speed internet?

No. Aether uses your local network (Wi-Fi or LAN) bandwidth. The transfer speed depends on your router's speed, not your ISP's internet plan.
